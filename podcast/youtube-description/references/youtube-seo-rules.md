# YouTube SEO rules

Limits marked **YouTube rule** come from YouTube's own product rules. Everything else is a working rule of thumb from publishing an interview podcast. YouTube does not publish how it ranks videos, so treat those as habits to test on your own channel.

## What the description is for

Click-through and watch time decide most of a video's reach, and the title and thumbnail drive the click. The description gives YouTube context on the topic, and its first two lines help the viewer decide.

## Above the fold

The first 125 or so characters show before "Show more".

- Get the guest's name and topic into the first 25 words.
- Be specific: "Dana Reyes on replacing a wholesale account worth 60% of revenue" beats "a discussion with a coffee industry leader".
- Save "welcome to another episode" for never.

## Keyword strategy

**Primary** (title, first 2 lines, and at least 2 tags): the guest's full name, their company, the headline topic and the episode's specific angle.

**Secondary** (2 to 4 natural mentions across the body): sector terms, audience descriptors and the show name.

Read it aloud. If it sounds stuffed, it is.

## Tags and hashtags are different things

| | Hashtags | Tags |
|---|----------|------|
| Where | In the description, last line | YouTube backend |
| Format | `#coffee #podcast` | `coffee podcast, coffee roasting, ...` |
| Visible to viewers | Yes | No |
| Quantity | 3 to 5 | 15 to 20 |

**Hashtag rules:**
- YouTube shows up to three above the title (**YouTube rule**), so put the best first.
- Mix broad and specific, and always include `show.hashtag`.
- Lowercase, no spaces.

**Tag rules:**
- 500 characters in total (**YouTube rule**).
- YouTube says tags play a small part in discovery, mostly helping with misspellings. Spend the effort on title and description first.
- Include common spelling variants and the guest's name.

**Tag template:**

```
[sector], [sector] podcast, [audience], [show name], [show name] podcast, [guest name], [guest company], [topic 1], [topic 2], [topic 3], [topic 4]
```

## Chapter timestamps

Chapters give viewers clickable jump points in the player, which helps them find the part they came for.

**YouTube rules:**
- First chapter at `00:00`.
- At least 3 chapters.
- Each at least 10 seconds long.
- Format: `MM:SS Title` or `HH:MM:SS Title`, one per line.

## Links

Link to the episode page on your site if you have one (`site.base_url` plus `site.episode_path`), the guest's LinkedIn and company, and any resource with a verified URL. Check every URL loads before publishing.

## Description length

Aim for 1,500 to 3,000 characters within YouTube's 5,000 limit (**YouTube rule**). Do not pad to reach it.

## What not to do

- An opener that only says what the episode covers.
- Pasting the whole transcript. It belongs on your episode page.
- Affiliate links in a guest's episode without saying so.
- "Smash that subscribe button."

## On a small channel

- Lean on topic search over show name, because few people know the show yet.
- Specific stories with numbers and named entities beat format pitches.
- Re-check these habits as the channel grows and people start searching for the show by name.
